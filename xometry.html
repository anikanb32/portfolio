Overview

Translating Figma designs to update the interface, streamlining the Request-For-Quote process, refactoring the codebase to React.

Xometry uses an AI platform to develop an online marketplace that connects clients with partners to source manufactured parts and assemblies.

Our work focused on streamlining the RFQ process which consists of the Manual Quotes workflow. Through this, Xometry staff sends quote requests to manufacturing partners on behalf of clients/buyers which can optimize the order and production.

As a frontend software developer, I worked along with other software development interns, a senior software engineer, product design interns, and a product manager. We communicated directly with product designers to work on design translations and design logistics. We also refactored the frontend repository in order to unify code and designs.

Problem

Managing outdated forms and cluttered information created significant inefficiencies in the RFQ process.

The existing RFQ system was originally designed for customer-facing interactions, but had evolved to serve primarily internal staff users. This fundamental mismatch created numerous pain points that significantly impacted daily operations and staff productivity.

Working closely with product designers and product managers, we identified several critical issues that were hindering efficient quote management: outdated multi-page forms creating unnecessary friction, information overload with scattered critical details across different interfaces, inconsistent user experience patterns that increased cognitive load, and limited real-time collaboration capabilities that delayed decision-making.

Through extensive collaboration with our product design team and product managers, we conducted user interviews and workflow analysis to understand the specific pain points staff faced daily. This close partnership was crucial in identifying not just the surface-level issues, but the underlying systemic problems that needed to be addressed.

The RFQ Process

Since the tool was mainly used by Xometry staff, but was created as customer-facing, there were several features that needed to be updated and re-evaluated. The RFQ form, Overview Page, and Partner Page was outdated and needed to be optimized for staff users.

Refactoring the Interface

Throughout this process, we converted the initial interface rendering which was in Javascript to React. Our goal was to make each component more intuitive and organized within the quoting repository and backend repository.

We communicated across teams to edit and refine the codebase as well as modifying designs based on feasibility of connecting the previous codebase with the updated version.

The refactoring process involved breaking down monolithic JavaScript components into reusable React components, implementing proper state management, and ensuring better code organization for maintainability and scalability.

Final Thoughts

Working at Xometry as a frontend development intern taught me the importance of cross-team collaboration and communication. I learned how to effectively translate design requirements into functional code while working closely with product designers and engineers. This experience gave me valuable insights into the full product development lifecycle, from design to implementation, and how user experience considerations must be balanced with technical constraints.
